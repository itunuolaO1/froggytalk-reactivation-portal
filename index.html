🚀 Staff Onboarding Guide: Outbound Reactivation Pilot

Welcome to the Outbound Reactivation Pilot! Thank you for volunteering to participate in this initiative. Your calls will help us reconnect with inactive customers, understand why they stopped using FroggyTalk, and encourage them to return to the app.

This guide explains how the programme works, what is expected of you, how incentives are paid, and where to find help during the pilot.

🎯 Programme Overview & Purpose

The Outbound Reactivation Pilot is a retention initiative designed to evaluate whether personalized outbound calls can successfully bring inactive customers back to FroggyTalk.

Primary Objectives:

1
Re-engage inactive customers and encourage them to return to FroggyTalk.
2
Encourage customers to update to the latest version of the app.
3
Increase successful reactivations and call credit purchases.
4
Gather insights on why customers stopped using FroggyTalk.
5
Evaluate whether outbound calling should become a long-term retention strategy.

✅ What Counts as a Verified Reactivation?

A customer is considered successfully reactivated only when all three of the following actions are completed:

Updates to the latest version of the FroggyTalk app (They need to update to get the latest app with the best functionality).
Makes a successful call using their allocated 2 free calling minutes.
Purchases call credit.
🤖 Once these actions are completed, the reactivation is automatically verified.

📞 Caller ID Masking

Why is my number being masked?

To protect your privacy and provide a consistent customer experience, every participant's phone number will be masked before the campaign begins.

Participants will be temporarily added to the FroggyTalk Customer Support calling plan.

As a result, customers will see a +31 (Netherlands) phone number instead of your personal number when you call them.

Key Privacy & Support Benefits
  • Your personal phone number remains private.
  • Customers do not call or message you directly after the campaign.
  • All future customer enquiries are routed through the official FroggyTalk Customer Support channels.
Restoring Your Caller ID

Once you have completed the campaign, or if you would like your original caller ID restored at any time, simply contact the Operations team.

The Operations team will remove you from the Customer Support calling plan and restore your normal caller ID.

💳 Payroll & Payment Timeline

All reactivation incentives are paid through your monthly salary.

On or Before 19th

Verified reactivations completed on or before the 19th of each month are included in that month's salary.

After 19th

Verified reactivations completed after the 19th are paid in the following month's salary.

Example: Any verified reactivations completed after 19 August will be paid in your September salary.

📋 Programme Rules

Do's
  • Contact assigned customers only.
  • Call between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM (customer local time).
  • Check it is a convenient time.
  • Keep conversations natural.
  • Update the customer's record in the Reactivation Portal immediately.
  • Maintain confidentiality.
  • Escalate technical bugs.
Don'ts
  • Do not contact customers outside your list.
  • Do not modify assignments.
  • Do not promise unapproved offers.
  • Do not share customer information.

📋 Pre-Call Checklist

Before making your first call, make sure everything below is ready.

The customer appears on your assigned list in the Reactivation Portal.
You have verified the customer's phone number.
You know the customer's last call destination.
Your FroggyTalk caller ID has been masked by Operations (Edikan).
You have sufficient FroggyTalk call credit to complete your assigned calls.
You are logged into the Reactivation Portal and ready to update customer records after every call.
The Calling Guide is open for quick reference (if needed).
You have reviewed the latest FroggyTalk promotions and pricing.
You have the Customer Support WhatsApp number available in case a customer needs additional assistance.
You understand the escalation process for issues you cannot resolve during the call.
You know the customer's preferred language.
You are calling between 08:00 AM and 5:00 PM in the customer's local time zone.
You're in a quiet environment with no distractions.

💡 Quick Calling Tips

Be Natural
  • Speak as though you're having a normal conversation.
  • Avoid sounding like you're reading a script.
  • Smile while talking—it naturally improves your tone of voice.
  • Pause naturally and don't rush through your sentences.
  • Build rapport before moving into the main purpose of the call.
Hook Early

Capture the customer's attention within the first few seconds.

Mention:

  • Who you are
  • Why you're calling
  • The free promotional minutes

Keep your introduction under 20 seconds before asking your first question.

Listen Actively
  • Let customers finish speaking before responding.
  • Listen carefully for objections and opportunities.
  • Acknowledge their concerns before offering a solution.
  • Ask follow-up questions where appropriate.
  • Never interrupt.
Tailor the Conversation

Don't give every customer the exact same conversation.

Instead:

  • Adapt based on their previous calling habits.
  • Mention destination-specific promotions where relevant.
  • If they mention pricing, explain current offers.
  • Personalise the conversation whenever possible.
Timing

Respect the customer's time.

Best practices:

  • Always work with the Timezones and call only between 5PM - 8PM Weekdays.
  • Avoid keeping customers on the phone unnecessarily.
  • Aim for around 2–5 minutes unless the customer wishes to continue.
  • If they're busy, ask whether there's a better time to call.
  • Never pressure customers into staying on the call.
Polite Finish

Always end every conversation professionally.

  • Thank the customer for their time.
  • Leave the conversation on a positive note.
  • Invite them back if their needs change.
  • Even if they decline today, ensure they leave with a positive impression of FroggyTalk.
Offer WhatsApp Support

If the customer needs additional help or has questions that cannot be resolved during the call:
